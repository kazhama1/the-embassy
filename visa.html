About Zambian Visas

A Zambian visa is an official authorization issued by the Government of the Republic of Zambia that allows eligible foreign nationals to travel to a Zambian port of entry and request admission into the country. The final decision to grant entry rests with an Immigration Officer upon arrival. Zambia welcomes visitors from around the world for tourism, business, investment, official government engagements, education, family visits, transit, and other lawful purposes. Depending on your nationality and the purpose of your visit, you may be eligible for visa-free entry, a visa on arrival, or you may be required to obtain a visa before travelling. It is the responsibility of every traveller to ensure that they meet the applicable entry requirements before beginning their journey. Applicants should also ensure that their passport remains valid for at least six (6) months beyond their intended date of entry and contains sufficient blank visa pages.

Visa Categories

Single Entry Visa

The Single Entry Visa is intended for travellers who plan to enter Zambia only once during the validity of the visa. Once the visa holder departs Zambia, the visa becomes invalid, even if there is remaining validity on the visa.

This visa is suitable for visitors travelling for:

  • Tourism and holidays
  • Family or private visits
  • Business meetings
  • Conferences and seminars
  • Religious activities
  • Short-term official visits
  • Medical treatment
  • Other temporary lawful purposes

Key Features:

  • Permits one entry into Zambia.
  • Valid for 90 days from the date of issue for entry.
  • Once used, the visa cannot be reused after departure.
  • The period of validity refers to the time during which the holder may enter Zambia, not the length of stay permitted in the country.
  • The authorized period of stay is determined by the Immigration Officer at the port of entry.

Typical Supporting Documents:

  • Valid passport
  • Completed visa application
  • Passport-size photograph (where required)
  • Travel itinerary or return ticket
  • Hotel reservation or proof of accommodation
  • Invitation letter (if visiting family, friends, or a business)
  • Proof of sufficient financial means, if requested

Double Entry Visa

The Double Entry Visa is issued to travellers who expect to enter Zambia twice during the validity of the visa. It is ideal for visitors whose travel itinerary requires leaving Zambia and returning before completing their journey. For example, a tourist visiting Zambia, travelling to a neighbouring country, and then returning to Zambia before departing home may benefit from this visa.

Key Features:

  • Allows two separate entries into Zambia.
  • Valid for 90 days from the date of issue for entry.
  • Expires automatically after the second entry has been used.
  • Suitable for regional travel involving neighbouring countries.

Uses:

  • Multi-country tourism
  • Regional business travel
  • Family visits involving neighbouring countries
  • Attendance at events held in more than one country

Multiple Entry Visa

The Multiple Entry Visa is designed for travellers who need to enter and leave Zambia several times during the visa's validity period. It is especially useful for frequent business travellers, investors, consultants, development partners, and visitors with ongoing commitments in Zambia.

Key Features:

  • Permits unlimited entries during the validity period.
  • Standard validity is 90 days from the date of issue for most eligible applicants.
  • For certain nationalities, including citizens of the United States under reciprocal arrangements, longer validity periods may apply.
  • Multiple Entry Visas are generally issued through the Zambia Department of Immigration's e-Visa system.

Suitable For:

  • Frequent business travellers
  • Investors
  • International consultants
  • NGO personnel
  • Regional project staff
  • Visitors making several trips within a short period
Applicants should note that possessing a Multiple Entry Visa does not authorize employment or residence in Zambia.

Transit Visa

A Transit Visa is issued to travellers who are passing through Zambia en route to another destination and who require a visa to enter Zambia. Transit visas are primarily intended for overland travellers travelling through Zambia to neighbouring countries.

Key Features

  • Intended solely for transit.
  • Maximum validity of seven (7) days.
  • Does not permit employment or long-term stay.
    • Applicants should possess proof of onward travel to their final destination.

Typical Supporting Documents

  • Valid passport
  • Confirmed onward travel ticket or itinerary
  • Visa for the next destination (where required)
  • Evidence explaining the transit route
Travellers wishing to remain in Zambia beyond the permitted transit period should apply for the appropriate visitor visa before travelling.

Day Tripper Visa

The Day Tripper Visa is intended for tourists making a short visit to Zambia lasting less than twenty-four (24) hours. It is commonly used by visitors staying in neighbouring countries who wish to make a brief excursion into Zambia, particularly to visit attractions such as Victoria Falls.

Key Features

  • Valid for visits of less than 24 hours.
  • Entry and exit must normally occur through the same designated border post.
  • Issued only at selected ports of entry.
  • Not available through the online visa application system.
  • Currently available at Victoria Falls Border Control and Kazungula Border Control.

Suitable For

  • Day tours
  • Cross-border sightseeing
  • Short tourism excursions
  • Visitors returning to the neighbouring country on the same day

Gratis Visa

A Gratis Visa is issued without payment of visa fees to eligible persons in accordance with Zambian immigration laws, government policy, or international agreements. Eligibility is determined solely by the Zambia Department of Immigration or an authorised Zambian Mission abroad.

Persons Who Qualify

  • Holders of diplomatic or official passports
  • Government officials travelling on official duty
  • Representatives of recognised international organisations
  • Individuals covered under bilateral or multilateral agreements
  • Other persons approved by the Government of the Republic of Zambia
Applicants may be required to provide official diplomatic notes, government letters, or other documentation supporting their entitlement to a Gratis Visa. The issuance of a Gratis Visa remains entirely at the discretion of the Zambian immigration authorities and does not exempt travellers from complying with other immigration requirements.

General Requirements

  • - Completed Visa Application Form -
  • - Passport valid for at least six months -
  • - Recent passport-size photograph -
  • - Travel itinerary or flight reservation -
  • - Proof of accommodation -
  • - Proof of sufficient financial means -
  • - Invitation letter (where applicable) -
  • - Supporting documents depending on visa category -

Processing Time

Visa applications are generally processed within 5 to 10 working days. Processing times may vary depending on the application type and supporting documentation.

Visa Fees

Visa fees vary depending on nationality, visa category and number of entries. Applicants should contact the High Commission or consult the latest fee schedule before submitting their application.

Important Information

  • - Incomplete applications may be delayed or rejected. -
  • - Submission of an application does not guarantee visa approval. -
  • - Applicants may be requested to attend an interview. -
  • - Additional supporting documents may be requested. -
  • - Ensure your passport has sufficient blank pages. -
  • - Travel insurance is strongly recommended. -
