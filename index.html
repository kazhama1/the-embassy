Welcome to the Zambia High Commission in the United Kingdom

Thank you for visiting the official website of the High Commission of the Republic of Zambia in the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland (UK). We are pleased to welcome you and hope that your visit to our platform provides you with clear, helpful, and up-to-date information about Zambia and the work of the High Commission. Whether you are planning to visit Zambia for tourism, business, education, or personal reasons, are seeking to explore economic and investment opportunities, or are simply interested in learning more about the activities and services of the High Commission, we hope you will find the information presented here both relevant and useful. Our aim is to serve as a reliable bridge between Zambia and the United Kingdom, ensuring that all visitors and stakeholders are well informed and supported. In line with our commitment to service excellence, the High Commission places humanity at the centre of all its activities, particularly in the delivery of consular and diplomatic services. Our staff are dedicated to providing assistance with professionalism, efficiency, and respect, ensuring that every client interaction reflects the highest standards of integrity and care. These values are deeply rooted in the principles of the Republic of Zambia and reflect our shared aspirations for a democratic, inclusive, and prosperous society. The High Commission in the United Kingdom plays a vital role in strengthening and deepening the longstanding bilateral relations between Zambia and the United Kingdom. Through continuous and constructive engagement with government institutions, private sector actors, academic bodies, and the wider community, we work to enhance cooperation in areas of mutual interest such as trade, investment, education, culture, and development. These partnerships continue to grow and contribute positively to the shared goals of both nations. Zambia’s democracy continues to mature and evolve, supported by strong constitutional institutions, a vibrant civil society, and the active participation of its citizens. While we recognize that challenges remain in our national development journey, we are equally encouraged by the progress that has been made over the years. Zambia remains firmly committed to peace, stability, good governance, and sustainable development, and we look forward to building on these foundations to create greater opportunities for all. Zambia is widely recognized as a premier tourist destination in Southern Africa, celebrated for its breathtaking landscapes, abundant wildlife, and rich cultural diversity. From the awe-inspiring Victoria Falls—one of the Seven Natural Wonders of the World—to its expansive national parks such as South Luangwa, Kafue, and Lower Zambezi, the country offers extraordinary experiences for nature lovers, adventure seekers, and cultural explorers alike. Visitors are also warmly welcomed by the renowned hospitality of the Zambian people, making every visit memorable and meaningful. In addition to its tourism appeal, Zambia is firmly open for business and investment. As one of Africa’s emerging economies, the country presents a wide range of opportunities across key sectors including mining, agriculture, energy, tourism, manufacturing, and information and communication technology. Zambia benefits from a stable and improving macroeconomic environment, a strategic location within the Southern African Development Community (SADC) region, and ongoing investments in infrastructure development. Combined with a young, dynamic, and hardworking population, these factors position Zambia as a promising destination for sustainable economic growth and international partnership. We invite you to explore this website further to learn more about our services, initiatives, and opportunities for collaboration with Zambia.
