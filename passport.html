Passport Services

The Zambia High Commission provides passport and travel document services to eligible Zambian citizens residing or travelling abroad.

Who Can Apply?

Passport services are available to Zambian citizens. Applicants must provide proof of citizenship together with all required supporting documents.







Passport Renewal

Renew an expired or soon-to-expire passport.

  • Current passport
  • Completed application form
  • Passport photographs
  • Applicable fee
Book Appointment

First-Time Passport

Apply for your first Zambian passport.

  • National Registration Card (NRC)
  • Birth Certificate
  • Passport photographs
  • Completed application form
Book Appointment

Lost Passport

Replace a lost or stolen passport.

  • Police Report
  • National Registration Card
  • Passport photographs
  • Completed declaration
Book Appointment

Damaged Passport

Replace a passport that has been damaged beyond normal wear.

  • Damaged passport
  • Completed application
  • Passport photographs
Book Appointment

Emergency Travel Document (ETD)

Issued to eligible Zambian citizens who urgently need to travel and do not possess a valid passport.

  • Proof of identity
  • Travel itinerary
  • Passport photograph
Book Appointment

Child Passport

Passport applications for children under the age of 16.

  • Birth Certificate
  • Parents' passports
  • Consent forms
  • Passport photographs
Book Appointment

Application Process

  1. Complete the passport application form.
  2. Gather all supporting documents.
  3. Book an appointment.
  4. Attend your appointment with original documents.
  5. Pay the applicable fees.
  6. Your application will be forwarded for processing.
  7. You will be notified when your passport is ready for collection.

Passport Fees

Service Fee (USD)

Replacement - Damaged Passport $150

Important: Passport fees are subject to change without prior notice. Please contact the High Commission to confirm the latest fees before submitting your application.
