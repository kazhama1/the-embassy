Immigration Information

The Zambia High Commission provides immigration information and consular assistance to travellers, residents and Zambian nationals. The Mission works closely with the Department of Immigration in Zambia to facilitate lawful travel and migration.

General Entry Requirements

  • Valid passport with at least six months validity.
  • Appropriate visa where required.
  • Confirmed travel itinerary.
  • Proof of accommodation.
  • Evidence of sufficient financial means.
  • Supporting documents relevant to the purpose of travel.
  • Compliance with applicable public health requirements.

Important Immigration Information

  • Immigration decisions are made by the competent authorities in Zambia.
  • Providing false information may result in refusal of entry or removal.
  • Visitors must comply with the conditions of their visas.
  • Overstaying a visa may result in penalties under Zambian immigration laws.
  • Employment is not permitted unless the appropriate authorization has been granted.
  • Residence and work permits must be approved before commencing employment where required.

Customs & Border Guidance

Travellers should declare all goods where required and comply with Zambian customs regulations. Restricted or prohibited items may be subject to inspection, seizure or other legal action.

  • Declare currency where required by law.
  • Carry prescribed medication with supporting documentation.
  • Avoid transporting prohibited goods.
  • Retain copies of important travel documents.

Emergency Assistance

If you are a Zambian national requiring urgent consular assistance, please contact the High Commission immediately.
